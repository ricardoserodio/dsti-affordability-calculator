# Disclaimer

## Educational and Demonstrative Purpose

This project is provided for educational, demonstrative and portfolio purposes only.

The **DSTI Affordability Calculator** is designed to help users understand general financial affordability concepts, including debt service, monthly income, existing credit commitments, financial effort and simple stress scenarios.

This project does not provide financial advice, credit advice, investment advice, lending recommendations or personal financial planning guidance.

---

## Not a Credit Approval System

This tool is not a credit approval system, underwriting engine, lending decision model or commercial credit assessment tool.

The outputs generated by this calculator must not be interpreted as:

- credit approval;
- credit rejection;
- eligibility confirmation;
- affordability confirmation;
- a lending recommendation;
- a guarantee of financing;
- a representation of any bank's internal decision process.

Any result produced by this project is purely indicative and based only on simplified assumptions configured for the simulation.

---

## No Replacement for Human Credit Analysis

This project does not replace formal human credit analysis, professional judgement, banking verification processes or institution-specific credit assessment.

Real credit decisions may consider many additional factors that are not included in this project, such as:

- full income verification;
- employment stability;
- credit history;
- collateral assessment;
- property valuation;
- legal documentation;
- risk profile;
- regulatory requirements;
- internal credit policies;
- macroeconomic conditions;
- institution-specific criteria.

This tool is intended to support understanding and explainability, not decision-making.

---

## Simplified Assumptions

The calculations used in this project are simplified and may not reflect the full complexity of real affordability assessments.

The project may include assumptions related to:

- monthly net income;
- existing credit commitments;
- proposed instalments;
- stressed instalments;
- DSTI thresholds;
- loan maturity;
- interest rate stress;
- loan-to-value simulation;
- scenario comparison.

These assumptions are configurable and educational. They should not be interpreted as official rules, regulatory limits or bank-specific criteria.

---

## No Use of Real Client Data

This project must not use real client data, confidential banking information, internal credit policies or proprietary templates.

All examples, inputs and scenarios should use:

- fictional names;
- fictional identifiers;
- simulated income values;
- simulated credit commitments;
- anonymised or fictional financial information;
- educational assumptions only.

Users should not enter real personal, financial or sensitive information into this tool.
